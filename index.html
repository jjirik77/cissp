Think like a manager, not a technician. Exam Blueprint

Domain Weights at a Glance

The exam draws from eight domains under the outline effective April 15, 2027. The weights are nearly flat: no domain is below 11 percent or above 14 percent. Every domain matters, so the schedule gives each one real time and the readiness radar in Lesson 9 shows where to spend the rest.

D1: Security Governance and Risk Management13.6%
D2: Asset Security12%
D3: Security Architecture and Engineering12.8%
D4: Communication and Network Security12.8%
D5: Identity and Access Management13.6%
D6: Security Assessment and Testing11.2%
D7: Security Operations12.8%
D8: Software Development Security11.2%

What this outline emphasizes. Ethics in AI and AI threats, post-quantum cryptography, OT and ICS, passwordless authentication and risk-adaptable access control, SOAR and EDR, immutable backups and recovery metrics, and the Secure Software Development Framework and SBOM requirements. Each lesson teaches these where they belong.

About This Workshop

Built for Faculty and Program Leads

Who this is for

This workshop is for community college faculty and program leads who are preparing for the CISSP exam and who want to bring its eight domains into their courses. It assumes working knowledge of security fundamentals and focuses on the judgment the exam rewards.

Each lesson pairs exam preparation with a faculty teaching segment: a signature assignment, rubric criteria, a lab idea, and the CAE-CD Knowledge Units the domain supports. eMates in every lesson give you short interactive refreshers, and many of them are ready to use with students.

How to use this resource

Work through the lessons in order. Lesson 0 explains the exam and the mindset. Lessons 1 to 8 follow the domains. Lesson 9 brings them together with exam strategy, a readiness profile, and a curriculum map. Participants also receive the official ISC2 study materials; this site complements them and does not reproduce them.
